xSSH is a modern SSH client for Android built for people who care about their data.

• Uses your phone's own keyboard — no built-in keyboard to fight with.
• Optional slim modifier bar for Ctrl, Alt, Esc, Tab, arrows.
• Private keys stored in Android Keystore, unlocked by biometric.
• Modern crypto only — legacy algorithms are refused at startup.
• Strict host-key checking. Screen-recording protection.
• 100% free. No subscription, no ads, no analytics. Apache 2.0.

Source: https://github.com/YOUR_ORG/xssh
